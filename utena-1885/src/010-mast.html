少女をとめ革命かくめいウテナ 鳳雛子 作

An alternate history in twenty-one parts

Utena, 1885: the Meiji serial and the world it made

What if all thirty-nine episodes of Revolutionary Girl Utena had first appeared as a pseudonymous illustrated serial in a Tokyo newspaper in 1885, four years before Japan wrote its constitution and thirteen before it wrote its family law? This is the history of that serial, from its first installment to 2026.

Written October 2026. Real history is marked documented; what follows from it, inference; where I picked one outcome among several, choice.
