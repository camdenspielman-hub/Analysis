An alternate history written in October 2026. The serial, its author, its editions and everything marked choice are invented; everything marked documented is real and sourced in Part XVIII. Revolutionary Girl Utena (1997) belongs to Be-Papas, Chiho Saito, Shogakukan, Shokaku Iinkai and TV Tokyo; nothing from its songs is reproduced here, and nothing from its scripts beyond a few short lines of dialogue quoted in Parts IV and VI, with a few words of its makers' published statements, attributed where they appear. The Japanese lines of the imagined serial are new writing.
